Privacy Policy

Charge Zone London · Last updated 24 October 2026

Charge Zone London ("the app") is made by Rinat Maltsev. In short: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer. Your trips, vehicles and location stay on your iPhone.

Your trips and vehicles

The vehicles, trips and payment records you add are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, which its widgets also read. If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends your starred vehicle and the days you still have to pay directly to your Watch. None of it is uploaded anywhere. If you export your trips as a CSV file, you choose where it goes.

Location

The map can show where you are, and zone alerts (off until you turn them on) use your location to notice when you're in the Congestion Charge zone. Your location is processed only on your iPhone: the app asks iOS to wake it near the zone and follows your position only while you're close to it. Locations are never stored beyond the day's trip (the time and place you entered the zone) and are never sent to the developer.

Motion & Fitness

If you allow it, the app asks iOS whether your iPhone was recently in a vehicle, so walks and bike rides through the zone don't count. This happens on your iPhone and nothing is stored or sent.

Searching and routes

When you search for a place, check a route or look for parking, your search, the route's start and end, and your approximate area go to Apple Maps (MapKit), as they would in the Maps app. Apple's privacy policy applies. The developer doesn't receive them.

Notifications and Siri

Zone alerts and payment reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Siri and Shortcuts requests run through Apple's services under Apple's privacy policy; the app answers them on your iPhone.

Links to TfL

Buttons such as “Pay on TfL's website” open Transport for London's website in your browser. The app isn't affiliated with TfL and doesn't send TfL any information; TfL's own privacy policy applies to its site.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and doesn't use the advertising identifier.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete trips and vehicles in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Turn off location access any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
