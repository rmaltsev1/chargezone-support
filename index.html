Charge Zone London

Know before you drive into the Congestion Charge zone

What it does

  • Check whether any address, postcode or place is inside London's Congestion Charge zone, and what it costs when you arrive.
  • Check a driving route before you go, with parking just outside the zone and the walk in.
  • Optional alerts when you drive into the zone during charging hours, even with the app closed.
  • Payment deadlines and reminders, so you pay the standard price and never get a penalty notice.
  • Your real price with electric, residents', Blue Badge and Auto Pay discounts, and an ULEZ estimate.
  • Widgets, Siri, Apple Watch and a Live Activity while you're in the zone.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay the Congestion Charge in the app?

No. Charge Zone London isn't made by or affiliated with Transport for London. Pay on TfL's website or app, then mark the day as paid in Charge Zone. The app links straight to TfL's payment page.

How do zone alerts work?

Turn on Zone alerts in Settings and allow location “Always”. Your iPhone wakes the app as you get close to the zone and follows your position only while you're near it. If you're in the zone during charging hours, you get one alert that day and the day is logged. Allowing Motion & Fitness lets the app ignore walks and bike rides.

I got an alert but wasn't driving.

Press and hold the alert and choose I wasn't driving, or swipe the day away in Trips.

Are the prices and hours up to date?

The app follows TfL's published rules: £18 a day (£21 if paid late, by midnight of the third day after travel), 07:00–18:00 on weekdays and 12:00–18:00 at weekends and on bank holidays, with no charge from Christmas Day to New Year's Day. Rules can change, so always check tfl.gov.uk before you travel.

Is my car ULEZ compliant?

The app estimates it from the fuel type and Euro standard you enter. TfL's vehicle checker has the final word.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it). There's no account and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.
